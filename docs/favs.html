My Orders

When I got to coffee shops, I have to get a drink to energize in order to tackle my work. Depending on my mood and weather, my orders typically fluctuate between a:

  • Cappuccino with almond or cocounut milk

  • Cold brew with a splash of coconut milk

  • Iced matcha with almond milk (+ lavendar syrup)

  • Matcha lemonade

  • Iced latte with coconut milk
